INSTITUTION ACCESS AND LOGGING POLICY (illustrative; contains deliberate defects)

PRINCIPLES
§1 Purpose: this policy protects the confidentiality and integrity of institutional data. It applies to all staff, contractors and service accounts.

ACCESS
§2 Staff are granted read access to the /finance share at all times.
§3 Contractors are granted access to the HR database only through the VPN.
§4 Service accounts must use unique credentials and must not share passwords.
§5 Administrators are allowed to enable remote desktop on any server.
§6 The public website bucket is open to anonymous read access.
§7 All access to the /finance share is denied outside business hours.
§8 Remote desktop must be disabled on every server that holds personal data.
§9 Personal data must never be stored in a public bucket.

CONTROLS
§10 Every access to the /finance share is logged and retained for 365 days.
§11 Passwords for service accounts are rotated every 90 days.
§12 Firewall rules block inbound traffic to the HR database from the internet.

ASSETS
§13 Assets in scope: the /finance share, the HR database, the public website bucket, all servers and all service accounts.
