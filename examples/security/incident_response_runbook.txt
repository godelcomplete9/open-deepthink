INCIDENT RESPONSE RUNBOOK (illustrative; contains deliberate coverage gaps)

SCOPE
1. Purpose: restore service after a security incident affecting customer-facing systems. This runbook applies to the operations team.

RESPONSE
2. The on-call engineer must execute the containment playbook within 30 minutes of a confirmed incident.
3. Affected hosts shall be patched and rebuilt from golden images before they return to production.
4. The incident commander must produce a timeline and a remediation report within 5 working days.
5. Compromised credentials must be revoked and reissued.

RECOVERY
6. Backups of customer databases are restored from the most recent clean snapshot.
7. Service owners verify restored data against checksums before reopening traffic.

ASSETS
8. Systems in scope: payment API servers, customer database cluster, load balancers, the build pipeline.
