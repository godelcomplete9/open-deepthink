NETWORK SEGMENTATION STANDARD (illustrative; contains deliberate defects)

PRINCIPLES
1. Objective: limit lateral movement so that a compromised workstation cannot reach production systems. This standard applies to every network zone.

ZONES
2. The management VLAN is open to all traffic from the office network for convenience.
3. Inbound connections to the management VLAN are prohibited except from the jump host.
4. Production databases accept connections only from the application tier.
5. Guest Wi-Fi is always allowed to reach the printers on the office network.
6. The office network must never route traffic from guest Wi-Fi.

CONTROLS
7. East-west traffic between zones is logged at the core firewall and reviewed weekly.
8. Firewall rule changes require a ticket and approval from the network owner.
9. Intrusion detection sensors monitor the production zone and alert the security team.

ASSETS
10. Zones: office network, guest Wi-Fi, management VLAN, application tier, production databases, jump host.
